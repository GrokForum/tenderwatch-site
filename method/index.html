Sources and method (DRAFT)

TenderWatch SA is a DRAFT built only from the public record and presented as found. Award data comes from the National Treasury eTenders portal and from the City of Cape Town's published award registers, each cited to its document. A company's legal form comes from its name, which the Companies Act requires to end with an expression such as "(Pty) Ltd" (s11(3)(c)), and every company must print its name and registration number on its notices and publications (s32(4)). The Companies and Intellectual Property Commission must make the information in its registers efficiently and effectively available to the public (Companies Act s187(4)(c); Companies Regulations, 2011, reg 174(1)(b)). Indicators are patterns in the public record, not findings about anyone. Private individuals are not identified.

Sources

  • National Treasury eTenders OCDS data, via the Open Contracting data registry (publication 143), retrieved 2026-10-19. Attribution: National Treasury, Office of the Chief Procurement Officer. Publication policy: eTenders "Learn more" (the portal also states Creative Commons BY 4.0).
  • City of Cape Town, tenders awarded registers (monthly PDFs, each linked on the rows it supports). This information resides at and is the property of the City of Cape Town.

Fact labels

  • Confirmed: from the award record itself, or from a City register row that shares the tender reference.
  • Reported, Unconfirmed: stated by a source, or linked by name and buyer only, and not confirmed by a second independent detail.
  • Unknown: no source gives it.

What each row shows

  • A supplier is named only when a source gives its legal form (for example "(Pty) Ltd" or "CC"). The name carries its fact label.
  • A name with no legal-form suffix in any source shows "Supplier name pending check" until a two-person check finds it is not a personal name.
  • A personal-looking name, and a company name held for privacy, is masked to a fixed 7 characters: its first character, five asterisks and its last character. A masked row shows only the buyer, award date and usable value, cites the source at register level, and shows no tender reference, identifier, registration detail or indicator.
  • A row where the sources disagree on the name is withheld and counted until it is checked.
  • Registration year: Year: YYYY, Confirmed when a registration year is confirmed by two independent details; Year: ****, Reported, Unconfirmed when a year is tracked but not confirmed (no digit is shown); Year: Unknown otherwise; Year: not shown (masked row) on masked personal-looking rows.

Indicators

An indicator is a pattern in the public record that may merit a closer look. It is not a finding that anyone did anything wrong. Every indicator links to its sources, and each supplier named has a right of reply ("Right of reply: pending").

  • I1, repeat awards to one supplier: the number of awards and the total usable value to the same supplier from one buyer in one municipal financial year (1 July to 30 June). Shown for 2 or more awards; highlighted at 3 or more. Shown only on rows where the supplier is named.
  • Indicators I2 to I7 are defined but not yet shown: each needs a source this draft does not hold.
