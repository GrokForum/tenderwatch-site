Privacy and contact

1. What this site is

TenderWatch SA is a draft register of South African government tender awards. It shows indicators, not findings. It is built only from official public records.

2. What data it shows

  • Tender award records as National Treasury's eTenders portal and the City of Cape Town publish them, each with a link to its source (see Sources and method).
  • A supplier is named only when a source shows it is a company or another juristic person.
  • A personal-looking name is masked: its first character, five asterisks, then its last character. Some company names are masked the same way.
  • The site never shows ID numbers, home addresses, personal phone numbers or personal e-mail addresses.

3. Purpose and basis

The purpose is public accountability: to help people follow how public money is awarded.

The site processes only information that public bodies have already published in official public records, and shows it as found, with its source.

The lawful basis is the processing of information in public records that the data subject or a public body made public, for this accountability purpose [legal wording unverified].

4. What the site does not do

The site uses no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no scripts. It has no forms and no accounts.

5. Corrections, removal and right of reply

To ask for a correction or a removal, or to reply about a record, send an e-mail to privacy@grokforum.site.

Identify the record by its award date and its tender reference or, where no reference is shown (masked rows), its buyer and usable value. Do not send ID numbers, phone numbers or addresses.

6. How long data stays

Each build replaces the published files. Earlier versions stay in the site's version history.

7. Who is responsible

The responsible party is GrokForum. Contact: privacy@grokforum.site.

8. Your rights

You can ask whether we hold information about you, and ask for a copy of it, by e-mail to privacy@grokforum.site.

You can ask for a correction of your information, or object to its processing, by e-mail to privacy@grokforum.site.

You can also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).
